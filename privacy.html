Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 18, 2026

Welcome to Cherish Habit Spark ("Sprout", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application treats your information when you use our app.

1. Information Collection and Storage

Cherish Habit Spark is designed with a privacy-first approach.

  • Local On-Device Data: Your habit entries, streak statistics, personal motivation notes, and timer preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit or store your personal habit records on external server databases.
  • No Account Required: You can use Cherish Habit Spark without creating an account or providing personal registration details such as your name or email address.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

Our app utilizes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers or pseudonymous advertising data to serve and measure ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

  • Google AdMob Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Permissions Requested

To provide core app functionality, Cherish Habit Spark requests the following device permissions:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to load app assets and display advertisements.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required to send local daily habit reminder notifications (only enabled if you opt in).

4. Children's Privacy

Cherish Habit Spark does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can immediately delete it.

5. Data Security

Since your habit records remain stored locally on your device, your data is as secure as your physical device. Deleting or clearing the app data from your device settings will clear your saved habits and progress.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
